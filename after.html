Ending & After Your Experience

Completing, Reflecting, and Translating Your Impact

Close your project responsibly, evaluate your outcomes, and articulate your real-world experience for your future career.

A project is not complete simply because the final deliverable has been handed over. Closing, reflecting, and translating your experience helps create lasting value for your partner while helping you understand your own professional growth.

Ending the Experience

Responsible offboarding helps ensure that your community partner or client can maintain, update, and benefit from your work after the project ends.

Complete and Transition Your Work

Package code, design files, reports, documentation, and other project assets into accessible and usable formats for your partner.

Close the Relationship Appropriately

Formally conclude the project with gratitude and clarity. Make sure your partner understands what has been delivered and establish clear boundaries for what happens after the project ends.

Evaluate Your Impact

Compare your final outcomes with the goals established at the beginning of the project and gather feedback from your client or community partner.

After the Experience

Your engaged learning experience can become part of your professional story. Reflect on what you learned and document your work so you can communicate your skills and impact to future employers.

Translate Your Experience

  • Articulate your skills: Turn complex project experiences into clear resume bullets and interview examples.
  • Build your portfolio: Document your process, important decisions, project artifacts, and measurable outcomes.
  • Maintain your network: Stay connected with mentors, community partners, clients, and teammates.
