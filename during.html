During Your Experience

Navigate Your Engaged Learning Experience

Once your project is underway, the focus shifts from preparation to managing the work effectively. During this phase, your team will conduct fieldwork and research, communicate with your sponsor, respond to feedback, and make adjustments as the project develops.

During the Experience

Engaged learning projects are active and collaborative. Your team should continue communicating with your partner, documenting decisions, and reflecting on whether the project is meeting both partner needs and your learning goals.

Fieldwork and Research

Use appropriate research and fieldwork methods to better understand the people, organization, or community connected to your project. Keep track of what you learn and use those findings to guide project decisions.

Keep Your Sponsor Updated

Maintain regular communication with your sponsor throughout the project. Share progress, ask questions, discuss challenges, and confirm any changes to the project scope or timeline.

Create Feedback Loops

Feedback should happen throughout the experience rather than only at the end. Give your partner opportunities to respond to work in progress and use their feedback to improve your team's decisions and deliverables.
